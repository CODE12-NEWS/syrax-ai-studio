Empowering Everyday Life with AI

DA NANG SEAPRODUCTS CORPORATION is a technology-driven company dedicated to building intelligent AI-powered mobile applications that help people make healthier, smarter, and more sustainable decisions every day. We believe that artificial intelligence should not be complicated—it should seamlessly integrate into everyday life, making knowledge accessible with just a simple camera scan.

Our mission is to transform advanced AI research into practical tools that improve daily living. By combining state-of-the-art computer vision, machine learning, and intuitive user experiences, we develop applications that empower users to better understand the world around them.

Our flagship AI solutions include:

  • AI Food Scan – Instantly analyze food, estimate nutritional values, identify ingredients, and provide healthier eating recommendations.
  • AI Tree & Plant Scan – Diagnose plant diseases, identify species, monitor plant health, and receive personalized care recommendations.
  • Natural Product Analysis – Discover information about fruits, vegetables, herbs, and other natural products through intelligent image recognition.
  • AI Lifestyle Assistant – Deliver personalized insights and recommendations that promote healthier habits and a more balanced lifestyle.

Our products are designed with simplicity and accessibility in mind. Users simply point their camera at an object, and within seconds, our AI delivers meaningful insights backed by advanced recognition technology. Whether identifying a plant, evaluating a meal, or learning about natural products, our applications make complex information easy to understand and act upon.

Innovation is at the heart of everything we build. We continuously invest in artificial intelligence, large-scale datasets, and cutting-edge mobile technologies to improve recognition accuracy, speed, and user experience. Every product is carefully designed to deliver reliable, intelligent, and user-friendly solutions for millions of people worldwide.

Beyond technology, we are committed to promoting healthier living and environmental awareness. By helping users better understand the food they eat and the plants they grow, we aim to encourage more sustainable choices and foster a stronger connection with nature.

As our AI ecosystem continues to grow, we remain focused on creating innovative applications that simplify everyday life, inspire healthier decisions, and unlock the full potential of artificial intelligence for everyone.
