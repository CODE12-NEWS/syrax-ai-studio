Request Account and Data Deletion

This page explains how to request deletion of your account and associated data for the AI Film Drama app.

How to Submit a Deletion Request

Email ducmin175214@gmail.com with the subject “AI Film Drama - Account Deletion Request.” Include the information needed to identify your account. Do not send your password or full payment details by email.

Delete Your Account in the App

  1. Open the Profile tab, then select Delete Account.
  2. Review the deletion notice and select Delete Account to confirm. Select Keep Account to cancel.

Account deletion is permanent. Your coins, watch history, and saved titles will be lost and cannot be recovered.
